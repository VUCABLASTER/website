Worum es geht

This is our first episode in English, because of our international guest Jurgen Appelo. You may know him from Management 3.0 or Managing for Happiness.

Today Roscoe and Manuel talking with him about how to implement agil in organizations, especially scaling it with his new model unFIX. Besides that, you'll get to know the person Jurgen Appelo better as well as his opinion to the current discussions on "agile is dead"

For more information about his new model, visit: https://unfix.work/

Have fun with this episode :)
